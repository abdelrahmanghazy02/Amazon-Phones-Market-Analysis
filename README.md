An end-to-end interactive Business Intelligence solution designed to analyze the e-commerce market for mobile phones on Amazon. This dashboard provides dual-perspective analytics: **Executive Business Performance** and **Customer Sentiment Intelligence**, wrapped in a sleek, Amazon-branded UI.

---

## 📌 Executive Summary

* **Gross Sales Volume:** **$25.42M** generated across **136K+ units** sold.
* **Net Sales Realized:** **$21.3M** after factoring in promotional discounts and returns.
* **Market Leader:** **Samsung** dominates the space, capturing **~$9.7M** (over 45% of total market revenue).
* **Customer Loyalty & Satisfaction:** Maintained a high **4.1 / 5.0 Average Customer Rating** across **882K+ reviews**.
* **Prime Logistics Impact:** **Prime-enabled products** drive **63.6% of sales volume** (560.9K units), proving fast shipping is a core purchase driver.

### 2️⃣ Executive Sales Overview
Focuses on operational metrics, revenue streams, inventory management, and price category segmentation.
* **Key Findings:**
  * **Premium ($250+) & Mid-Range ($100-$250)** segments drive the bulk of revenue ($9.1M & $7.8M respectively).
  * **Mid-Range Category** consumes the highest proportion of promotional discounts (**39.5%**).
  * Identified **$6.3M in inventory risk** tied to high-performing items labeled as *Limited Stock*.

### 3️⃣ Customer Intelligence
Focuses on the *Voice of the Customer (VoC)*, product feedback ratings, Prime impact, and risk management.
* **Key Findings:**
  * **Budget Segment (<$100)** drives the highest engagement with **433K reviews** (53.1% of total units sold).
  * **Risk Identification:** Isolated **22 high-risk products** (*Review Required* category) that threaten overall customer satisfaction.
  * Non-Prime items lose approximately **40% of potential sales opportunities**.

## 💡 Strategic Business Recommendations

1. **Inventory Mitigation:** Immediately secure stock for Samsung's top-selling models currently in the *$6.3M Limited Stock* bucket to prevent stockouts and revenue leakage.
2. **Quality Control & Risk Management:** Audit customer feedback for the **22 "Review Required" products** to improve supplier quality or delist items hurting the brand score.
3. **Prime Onboarding:** Encourage 3rd-party sellers in the non-Prime catalog ($321K units) to transition to FBA (Fulfilled by Amazon) to capture up to 40% more sales velocity.
4. **Discount Optimization:** Reallocate a portion of the *Mid-Range* discount budget to the *Budget* category to stimulate volume without eroding core margins.
